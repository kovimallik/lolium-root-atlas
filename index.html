      About

      Whole roots of two perennial ryegrass genotypes of contrasting field nitrogen-use efficiency (Barhoney, high; Fagerlin, low) were sampled 4 h after nitrogen resupply at full or 25% less N, after one week of starvation, three clonal replicates each (12 libraries, 10x GEM-X snRNA-seq).

      Nitrogen metabolism is partitioned into three cell states: high-affinity nitrate and ammonium uptake with primary assimilation in an outer-cortical state, amino-acid synthesis in the inner cortex, and amino-acid export (AAP transporters) at the pericycle. The high-NUE genotype runs the uptake state constitutively; the low-NUE genotype induces it.

      The schematic is drawn from general grass root anatomy (polyarch stele with a central metaxylem, multilayered cortex with an exodermis, closed meristem with columella and lateral root cap) and coloured by the cell states of this atlas; the number of cortical layers and xylem poles is schematic, and the radial placement of the two cortical nitrogen states follows marker genes and label transfer from the wheat and maize root atlases, pending in situ hybridisation. Phloem and root cap were not resolved as separate states in whole-root nuclei.
